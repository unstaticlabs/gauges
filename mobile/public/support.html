Gauges by Unstatic Labs

Gauges shows the chores that come back, as gauges that empty with time. You can share labels with the people you live with.

Contact

For a question, a problem, or an idea, write to gauges@unstaticlabs.com. Tell us your phone model or browser, and what you did when the problem happened.

Common questions

I see no data on the website

The website needs a login. Log in with the same Google or Apple login that you use in the phone app. On the phone, add the login in Settings, then Account.

I do not get reminders

Reminders work in the phone app only. Turn them on in Settings, and allow notifications for Gauges in the settings of your phone.

How do I share a label?

Open the label, then share it. The other person scans the QR code or opens the link.
