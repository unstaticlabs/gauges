Delete your Gauges account

Gauges by Unstatic Labs

You can delete your account and your data at any time. You can do it in the app, or by email if you do not have the app any more.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Gauges on your phone, or open the website and log in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. In Account, tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. The app can ask you to log in one more time.

The app deletes your data at once. You cannot undo this.

Delete by email, without the app

Write to gauges@unstaticlabs.com with the subject “Delete my Gauges account”. Send the email from the address of your Google or Apple login. If you used “Hide My Email” with Apple, give us the relay address that Apple made for Gauges. You find it in the settings of your Apple account, under Sign in with Apple.

We delete the account within one month, and we send you an email when it is done. We can ask you to confirm that the account is yours.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and your Google or Apple login on Gauges, with your email address.
  • Your name.
  • Your private labels, and the gauges that only you use.
  • Your membership of each shared label.

What stays

  • A gauge that you created and that other members use stays with them. Your account ID stays on it as its creator, but with no name.
  • Your check-offs on shared gauges stay in their history, with your account ID. Your name is deleted, so the other members no longer see who you are.
  • The record of each invite that you made stays in the database, unless a member of the label made a new link for it. It contains your account ID and two dates, but no name.
  • The reminder settings stay on your phone until you delete the app.

Google, which hosts the data, removes deleted data from its backups within about 6 months.

Without a login

On the phone, you can use Gauges without a login. Then only your phone can reach the account, and we cannot find it from an email. Delete the account in the app before you delete the app.

An account without a login that holds no gauges and no labels, and that nobody used for 30 days, needs no request. We delete these accounts regularly.
