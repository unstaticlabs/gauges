Privacy policy

First publication: 15 November 2026 · Last update: 15 November 2026

In short: Gauges keeps only what it needs to show your chores and to share them with the people that you choose. There are no ads, no tracking, and no sale of data. You can delete your account and your data in the app at any time.

Who is responsible for your data

Unstatic Labs publishes Gauges. Unstatic Labs decides why and how the app uses your data. Under the European data protection law (GDPR), this makes Unstatic Labs the “controller” of your data.

  • Unstatic Labs SAS, 60 rue François Ier, 75008 Paris, France
  • Email: gauges@unstaticlabs.com

Unstatic Labs has no data protection officer. The law does not require one for Gauges. For each question about your data, write to the email address above.

The legal notice gives the full details of the publisher and of the host.

What data the app keeps

DataWhere it comes from
An account ID The app makes a random ID for your account. On the phone, the app makes an account without a login at the first start. The website always needs a login. If your browser still has an account without a login from the old Gauges website, the new website logs it out and changes nothing in it.
Your email address and your profile name from Google or Apple Only when you log in with Google or Apple. Google or Apple sends this data to us when you log in. Apple can hide your real email address. Your check-offs The date and time each time that you mark a chore done, and who did it.
Your labels The name and the emoji of each label, its gauges, and the list of its members.

Account moves When you log in on a phone that already has gauges, and your login already has an account, you can move the gauges to that account. During the move, the server keeps a short record with the two account IDs. The app deletes the record after the move.

Your emails to us If you write to us, we keep your email address and your message to answer you.

You do not have to give a name or a login. Without a login, you cannot use the website or keep your gauges when you change phones. Without a name, you cannot share a label.

Data on your device

Some data stays on your device only and never goes to the server:

  • Your reminder settings: on or off, and the time.
  • Display choices, and the invite links that you showed recently.
  • The login session, so that you stay logged in.
  • On the phone, a copy of your gauges and labels, so that the app also works offline.

The website keeps these items in the storage of your browser. It uses no cookies for ads or for statistics. It keeps only what the service needs to work, so it does not ask for your consent.

When you log in with Google or Apple, a window of Google or Apple opens. Google or Apple can set their own cookies in that window, under their own privacy policies.

What the app does not do

  • The app shows no ads.
  • The app contains no analytics, tracking, or crash reporting tool.
  • The app does not sell or rent your data.
  • The app does not read your contacts, your location, or your photos.
  • The app makes no automated decisions about you.

Apple and Google can give app publishers statistics about downloads and crashes. They do this under their own privacy policies, and only if you allow it in the settings of your phone.

Camera

The phone app uses the camera only to scan the QR code of a shared label. The app asks for your permission first. The app does not record, keep, or send images.

Notifications

Reminders work in the phone app only, and they are off until you turn them on. Your phone makes the reminders itself. No server sends them, and the app sends no data to make them.

Shared labels

You can share a label with other people. Then the members of the label see:

  • your name,
  • the gauges that have this label,
  • the check-offs on these gauges, with the name of the person who did each one.

The apps of the members also receive your account ID, and the list of people who turned off the reminders of these gauges. The app does not show this list.

Other Gauges users can read your name if they know your account ID. The app shows names only to people who share a label with you. The members do not see your email address.

Who receives your data

Only Unstatic Labs and its host, Google, receive your data. The members of your shared labels see the data that the section “Shared labels” lists.

The app keeps the data in Google Firebase. Google acts as a processor for Unstatic Labs: it uses the data only on our instructions.

  • Cloud Firestore keeps your gauges, labels, check-offs, invites, names, and the records of account moves. Its database is in the Google region europe-west3 in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Firebase Authentication keeps your account ID and your login: your email address and your profile from Google or Apple. Google runs this service only in data centers in the United States.
  • Firebase Hosting delivers the website from the global network of Google.

If you log in with Apple, Apple confirms your login to us. If you log in with Google, Google does the same. They do this under their own privacy policies.

Transfers outside the European Union

Your login data goes to the United States, and the network of Firebase Hosting can deliver the website from other countries. Google LLC takes part in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The European Commission decided that this framework gives an adequate level of protection to the companies that take part. The data processing terms of Google also contain the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission.

You can read these terms: the Cloud Data Processing Addendum (for Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication) and the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms (for Firebase Hosting). You can also ask us for a copy.

How long we keep your data

  • We keep your data while your account exists.
  • On the phone, the app makes an account without a login at the first start. We regularly delete accounts without a login that hold no gauges and no labels, and that nobody used for 30 days, with their name.
  • An account with data stays until you delete it in the app, or until you ask us by email.
  • When you delete your account, the app at once deletes your account, your name, your private labels, and the gauges that only you use. It also removes you from the shared labels and gauges. Google then removes the deleted data from its backups within about 6 months.
  • Some shared data stays with the other members: a gauge that you created and that others use, and your check-offs on shared gauges. Your account ID stays on this data, but your name is deleted, so the other members no longer see who you are.
  • An invite stops working after 7 days. When a member of the label makes a new link for it, the app deletes the record of the old invite. Other records of invites stay in the database, also after you delete your account. A record contains the account ID that made it and two dates, but no name.
  • After you move gauges to another account, the old account without a login stays in the login service with no data and no name. The regular deletion of accounts without a login removes it after 30 days.
  • Google keeps IP addresses for a few weeks for the login service, and for a few months for the website.

On the phone, you can use the app without a login. If you delete the app, your data stays on the server, and you cannot open it again. So delete your data in the app before you delete the app.

Delete your account

In the app or on the website, open Settings, then Account, then tap Delete my account. Read how to delete your account for the details, and for the steps if you do not have the app any more.

Your rights

You have these rights on your data:

  • Access: ask for a copy of your data.
  • Correction: change your name in the app, or ask us to correct other data.
  • Deletion: delete your account in the app, or ask us by email.
  • Portability: ask for your data in a file that a computer can read.
  • Objection and restriction: ask us to stop or to limit a use of your data.
  • Instructions for after your death: tell us what to do with your data after your death.

To use a right, write to gauges@unstaticlabs.com from the email address of your login, if you have one. If you have no login, copy the support code in Settings (tap the version number) and add it to your email. We answer within one month.

You can also make a complaint to the French data protection authority, the Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés (CNIL), 3 place de Fontenoy, 75007 Paris, France: www.cnil.fr/fr/plaintes.

Children

Gauges is for a general audience. It is not made for children, and it asks for no age. If you think that a child under 15 uses Gauges without the agreement of a parent, write to us. We then delete the account.
